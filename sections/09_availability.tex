\section*{Data and Code Availability}
The scientific implementation, versioned protocol, and canonical replica-level evaluation records are available in the AQSE repository at the frozen revision cited in~\cite{aqse2026canonical}. Section~\ref{sec:methods-provenance} distinguishes the experiment, report-recovery, and publication revisions. The manuscript sources, plotting inputs, and report-only analysis script are available at \url{https://github.com/florianocaprio/AQSE-Paper}. The manuscript analysis operates on released scores and checkpoint diagnostics without new model fitting or access to test measurement arrays. The reported benchmark uses synthetic data, not field measurements.
